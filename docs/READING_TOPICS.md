# Expanded consultation questions

Reviewed September 17, 2026. These sources inform topic selection, not proof that palmistry can answer the questions accurately. They are a small qualitative sample, not a survey or frequency ranking.

- [Astrology Answers: meaningful questions](https://support.astrologyanswers.com/hc/en-us/articles/20034847862285-How-do-I-ask-an-advisor-meaningful-questions) groups consultation interests around relationships, career/finances, life changes and growth.
- [AstroNidan: consultation topics](https://astronidan.com/ask-astrologer/) lists work, partnership, family, property, education and travel among its question categories. Its efficacy claims are not adopted.
- [Wishastro palm-reading guide](https://wishastro.com/tools/palm-reading) highlights marriage, money and career as recurring palmistry interests. Its additional line/mount rules have not been added to our verified observation guide.

Seven deeper sections now follow the main reading: romance/commitment, friendships/social life, money/assets/security, family/home, career/business, learning/travel/change, and blind spots/watch-outs. Each has two specific questions, a concise overview, a conditional watch-out, a practical step and a server-owned scope statement. The aim is roughly 110–150 words per topic in addition to the core report, not padding to meet a guaranteed length.

These sections receive the existing report, not photos, and cannot add new visual observations. Topics beyond the bounded palm reference are explicitly general reflections. The money section cannot establish assets, income, inheritance or returns; relationship sections cannot establish status, fidelity or marriage timing. No medical, fertility or misfortune forecasting is introduced. A question's popularity does not make its premise valid.

Generation adds one guarded call to a normal reading and one additional rewrite call to a roast. Each remains capped at 3,000 output tokens so the existing $1-per-call reservation and budget accounting stay valid. New normal readings take three calls; new roast readings five. The paired test reuses one normal reading and also takes at most five. All paid testing remains under the cumulative $10 authorization.

The optional lifeAreas field preserves compatibility with old reports and the sample. New reports store the sections in the existing Supabase reading JSON; no new database or migration is needed. Downloaded Markdown includes all new questions and answers.
