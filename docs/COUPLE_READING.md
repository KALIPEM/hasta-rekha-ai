# Couple matching

Choose Couple matching at the top of the scanner. Male and female panels each require at least one labelled left/right photo, with both hands optional, and a dominance selector. Both partners must consent and be adults. Gender labels identify uploads, never determine palm features or personality. Unknown dominance stays unknown.

Three serial Azure requests: separate image inspection per partner, then a text-only comparison across emotional chemistry, communication, elemental temperament, commitment and affection. Every request goes through the existing Supabase budget reservation guard, with a 3,000-token maximum and no retries. Reports use the existing Supabase JSON reading storage (standard voice, Couple compatibility focus), library, share and download flow. No photos or local database are persisted. No schema migration is needed.

Server-owned evidence preserves partner, physical side and dominant/non-dominant labels. Missing hands and uncertain features cannot be treated as observations. Flat photos cannot determine mount firmness, vitality or intimacy. Relationship edge lines are often unavailable. No numerical compatibility, marriage/divorce verdict, fidelity prediction or intimate trait inference is requested. Traditional interpretations remain symbolic, not scientifically validated assessments.
